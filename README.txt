# Cowboy Scorekeeper PWA files

Files included:
- manifest.webmanifest
- icon-192.png
- icon-512.png
- index-head-snippet.html

How to use:
1. Copy `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon-192.png`, and `icon-512.png` into your project's `public/` folder.
2. Paste the contents of `index-head-snippet.html` inside the `<head>` of your `index.html`.
3. Build and deploy your Vite app.
4. On iPhone, open the deployed site in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen.
